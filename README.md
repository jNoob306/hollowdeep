# Hollowdeep

A procedurally generated 3D extraction roguelike in a single HTML file (Three.js from a CDN), with character models in `assets/`.

Enter the cave, mine and loot through winding maze-like tunnels, then find an exit shaft and climb out to keep your backpack. Die and the backpack is lost. Craft gear at camp and go deeper.

From depth 2, **the Bellman** stalks the tunnels: an unkillable figure whose bell rings with every step. It hunts by sound, its grab is fatal, and the only answer is to sneak, hide and run.

## Controls

**Xbox controller:** Left stick move (push lightly to sneak) · Right stick camera · RB light attack · RT heavy attack · B roll · LB block · X flask · A interact · R3 lock-on · flick right stick or D-pad left/right to switch target · View or Y map · Menu pause. Menus: D-pad to move, A select, B back, LB/RB change tab.

**Keyboard + mouse:** WASD · mouse look · left/right click attacks · Space roll · Shift block · hold C to sneak · Q lock-on · E interact · F flask · M map · P pause.

**Touch:** left side move stick, drag right side for camera, on-screen buttons.

**Map setting:** the pause menu and the camp's Controls tab have a Map button that cycles between the mini-map plus full map, the full map only (on the Map button), and no map at all.

Tip: open the Space's direct link (the full-screen "static.hf.space" address) if the controller or mouse look does not respond inside the embedded page.
